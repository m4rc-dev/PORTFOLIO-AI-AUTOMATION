AI lead qualifier + CRM updater

An n8n flow that takes inbound leads and scores them 1 to 10 with an LLM (Groq). Each contact is saved to HubSpot with the score, an AI summary and a suggested next step. Hot leads (7 and up) trigger an instant Telegram alert with a drafted reply. Retries and a manual-review fallback mean no lead is lost if the AI service is down or rate-limited.

n8n, Groq, HubSpot, Telegram, Webhooks, JavaScript
